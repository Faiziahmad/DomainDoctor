**Full description:**

> **Can someone pretend to be this business by email?**
>
> Open any business website, click DomainDoctor, and a check-up slip prints right in your browser: a score out of 100, a clear stamp (Protected, Partly protected or At risk), and a plain-English line for each setting.
>
> **What it checks**
> • Sender list: which services are allowed to send email for the business (SPF)
> • Fake-email rule: what happens to emails that pretend to be the business (DMARC)
> • Secure padlock: whether the website opens securely
> • Signed settings, padlock issuers and secure incoming mail: helpful extras
>
> Tap any line to see what it means and exactly how to fix it, with copy-paste examples.
>
> **Great for**
> • Business owners checking their own domain
> • Web designers and IT helpers checking a client's setup
> • Anyone who wants to know whether an email "from" a company could be fake
>
> **Private by design**
> • Only reads public information: the same records every email service reads
> • Only runs when you click it, and can't read your pages or history
> • No accounts, no tracking, nothing stored
>
> Made by Faizi Ahmad. Want it fixed for you? Use "Get it fixed" in the popup.
