# DomainDoctor extension: privacy policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

**Short version: DomainDoctor collects nothing, stores nothing and tracks nothing.**

## What happens when you click the extension

1. The extension reads the web address of the tab you're on. It can only do this when you click its icon (Chrome's `activeTab` permission). It can't see the page's content, your history or other tabs.
2. It takes the main domain from that address (for example `shop.example.co.uk` becomes `example.co.uk`) and looks up that domain's **public DNS records** through Google Public DNS (`dns.google`), with Cloudflare DNS (`cloudflare-dns.com`) as a backup. These are the same public records every email service reads.
3. It visits the website's homepage once, like a normal visit, to see whether it opens securely (HTTPS).
4. It shows the results in the popup. When you close the popup, they're gone.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no advertising.
- Nothing is sent to DomainDoctor or Faizi Ahmad. There is no DomainDoctor server.
- No personal information is collected, sold or shared.
- We don't read, change or inject anything into the pages you visit.

The only thing saved is your light/dark mode choice, and it stays in your browser.

## Third parties

The DNS lookups go to Google Public DNS or Cloudflare DNS, whose own privacy policies apply:
- https://developers.google.com/speed/public-dns/privacy
- https://developers.cloudflare.com/1.1.1.1/privacy/public-dns-resolver/

If you click "Get it fixed", your own email app opens with a draft addressed to faizia995@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send it.
